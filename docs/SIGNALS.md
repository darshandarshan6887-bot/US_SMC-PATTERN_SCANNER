# Signal catalogue

Every signal the scanner can produce. **Direction** is bull / bear / neutral. **Reliability** is the Candlestick Pattern Handbook's own star rating for candlestick patterns (patterns the Handbook leaves unrated are given 2 stars, or 3 where it quotes 65-70%). It only feeds the confluence score; it is not a promise of success.

Candlestick sizes are measured against the 14-candle ATR. Trend context (needed by most reversal patterns) means a net move of at least 1 ATR over the 8 candles before the pattern.

### Relaxed for 1-hour charts
US stocks almost never gap inside the session, so these Handbook patterns that mention a gap only require the second candle to open **at or beyond the previous close**: Morning Star, Evening Star, Piercing Line, Dark Cloud Cover, Bullish/Bearish Meeting Lines, On-Neck, In-Neck, Thrusting.
These are gap patterns by definition and stay strict, so they mostly appear on the 9:30 candle: Kicker, Rising/Falling Window, Abandoned Baby, Tri-Star, Two Black Gapping, Mat Hold.

### Not separate detectors
- *Last Engulfing Pattern* is the Handbook's alternate name for Engulfing.
- *Counterattack Lines* is the same pattern as Meeting Lines (the Handbook says so).
- *Concealing Baby Swallow*: the Handbook lists it as bearish, but the classical definition (four black candles at the end of a downtrend) is a **bullish** bottom reversal. The scanner follows the classical definition.

## Compression, volume, trend and structure events

| Signal | Group | Direction | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inside Bar | range | neutral | High below and low above the previous candle's, previous candle at least 0.5 ATR tall. Kept for 7 candles. |
| NR4 | range | neutral | Narrowest range of the last 4 candles. Kept for 7 candles. |
| NR7 | range | neutral | Narrowest range of the last 7 candles. Kept for 7 candles. |
| Bollinger Squeeze Breakout | squeeze | bull / bear | Bollinger bandwidth was in its lowest 20% (last ~63 sessions) within the previous 3 candles, then the close breaks outside the band. |
| TTM Squeeze Fired | squeeze | bull / bear | Bollinger Bands were inside the Keltner Channel on the previous candle and are not now. Direction from the momentum histogram. |
| ATR Expansion Breakout | squeeze | bull / bear | ATR was in its lowest 20% within the previous 10 candles, then a candle with true range of 1.5x ATR or more closes beyond the 20-candle range. |
| Donchian Squeeze Breakout | squeeze | bull / bear | 20-candle channel width was in its lowest 20% within the previous 5 candles, then the close breaks the 20-candle high or low. |
| Volume Spike Breakout | volume | bull / bear | Volume dried up (median relative volume of 5 candles at 0.6 or less) within the previous 10 candles, then relative volume of 2.0 or more and a close beyond the 20-candle range. |
| Pocket Pivot | volume | bull only | Up candle above the 50-candle average (within 3 ATR) whose relative volume beats every down candle in the last 10. |
| ADX Trend Start | trend | bull / bear | ADX(14) crosses above 25 after being under 20 within the last 10 candles. Direction from +DI vs -DI. |
| MA Ribbon Fan-Out | trend | bull / bear | The 5/10/20/50 averages line up in order after the ribbon was in its tightest 20% within the last 15 candles, and the ribbon is widening. |
| Fair Value Gap | structure | bull / bear | Gap between candle 1's high and candle 3's low (or the reverse) of at least 0.25 ATR, with a strong middle candle. Status open / partial / filled. |
| Turtle Soup | structure | bull / bear | Price sweeps the 20-candle high or low and closes back inside (same candle, or the next one). |
| Undercut & Rally | structure | bull only | Within 3 candles of undercutting the 40-candle low, a green candle on relative volume of 1.2 or more closes back above it. Bullish only. |
| Ichimoku Cloud Twist | structure | bull / bear | Span A crosses Span B (9/26/52 on 1-hour candles). The note says whether price is above, inside or below the plotted cloud. |

## Current readings (stored once per symbol, shown on the stock page)

Bollinger squeeze, TTM squeeze, ATR contraction, historical-volatility contraction, volume dry-up, Donchian squeeze, MA-ribbon squeeze, OBV divergence, A/D-line divergence, CMF, ADX, Choppiness Index, price vs Ichimoku cloud, relative volume, ATR. The *compression layers* count is how many of the eight compression readings are on at once (the Pre-Breakout guide recommends 2 to 4 agreeing layers).

## Relative volume
Hourly volume is far heavier at the open and the close, so a candle's volume is compared with the average of the **same time-of-day candle over the previous 50 sessions**, not with the previous few candles.

## Confluence score (0-100)

| Part | Max | How |
|---|---|---|
| Pattern strength | 30 | Star rating / 5 x 30 (neutral signals x 0.6) |
| SMC alignment | 25 | Same direction as the SMC trend 25, opposite 9, SMC unknown 12, neutral 0 |
| Freshness | 20 | Full marks on the latest candle, falling to 0 at 20 candles |
| Volume | 15 | Relative volume 0.8x gives 0, 1.8x or more gives 15 |
| Squeeze | 10 | Squeeze fired 10, squeeze on 6 |

The score ranks setups against each other. It is **not** a probability of success.

## Confirmation (candlestick patterns)
The Handbook's golden rule is to wait for a later candle to close beyond the pattern. Each directional candlestick signal is marked **pending** (no later candle has decided it), **confirmed** (a later close beyond the pattern's high for bullish, low for bearish) or **failed** (a later close beyond the stop reference first).

## Confirmation in the pattern tables

The Bullish and Bearish pattern tables use the same confirmation rule as the SMC + pattern tables: a candle pattern only counts once a **later candle has closed beyond it** (the Candlestick Handbook's rule). Failed patterns are never shown.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Confirmed | A later candle closed beyond the pattern. |
| Pending | The pattern formed but no later candle has closed beyond it yet (always the case on the latest candle). |
| Trigger | Squeeze, volume, trend and structure events. The close beyond a level is the signal itself, so there is nothing left to confirm. |

Turtle Soup and Undercut & Rally are decided from the saved candles: a later close beyond the signal candle's extreme confirms them, a close the other way kills them, whichever comes first. The **Pattern confirmation** filter switches between *Confirmed only* (default, and what the Top 20 boards always use), *Pending only* and *Confirmed and pending*.
